Community Impact

A year of food for 55 children

Community Impact7 December 2023

The Alpha Kappa Alpha Eta Beta Omega Chapter chose Rosetta Steps as the pilot school for its Childhood Hunger Initiative.

Through the Power Packs programme, 55 of our children received food support across a full year — a pilot the chapter designed to test what consistent nutrition does for attendance and concentration in a Liberian community school.

Hunger is an academic problem

A child who has not eaten since yesterday is not thinking about phonics. Teachers see it in the first hour: heads down on desks, short tempers, children who cannot hold a pencil steady. Attendance also slips, because a hungry child has less reason to walk to school and less energy to stay once there.

Food support is therefore not separate from education. It is the precondition for it.

What the pilot delivered

  • Food support for 55 children over twelve months
  • Relief for families carrying the cost of a daily meal
  • A stable base for the school day, from morning assembly to last lesson

Teachers reported steadier mornings over the year, and families told us the packs changed what was possible at home: money that would have gone to food went instead to uniforms, books and transport.

Our kitchen always needs help

Rice, beans, oil and fresh produce are bought term by term. A gift of any size goes directly into a child’s plate.
