Learn Through Play: every Saturday, 9am to 12pm

EventsEvery week

Our gates open free of charge to children from the community for a morning of learning disguised as play.

Every Saturday from 9am to 12pm, children from Caldwell Road and the streets around us come onto campus for stories, games, songs and craft. No uniform, no fee, no registration desk — they simply arrive.

What happens in the three hours

  • Story circle — a book read aloud, with questions and voices
  • Word games — letter hunts, rhyming, matching and spelling races
  • Make and do — drawing, cutting, building with whatever is on hand
  • Play — because the point is that learning feels like something you want to come back to

Children who attend the Saturday sessions often arrive at school on Monday already knowing a few more letters than they did on Friday. For children who are not enrolled anywhere, the morning is sometimes the only structured learning in their week.

Why we run it free

Rosetta Steps began in 2014 with a reading room — a table, a few books and an open door. Learn Through Play is that idea, kept alive. Some of the children who now attend the school full-time first came to a Saturday session with an older sibling.

Volunteer on a Saturday

We need readers, craft helpers and people who are simply good at playing. A morning a month makes a difference.
