Sponsorship

Sponsor a child for a full year

For $280 a year — less than a dollar a day — you can cover what it costs to educate one child at Rosetta Steps. Some families can pay this. Most cannot, which is why scholarship sponsors are the backbone of this school.

Sponsors receive a termly update written by the school and a year-end note about the difference their gift made. You are not buying a relationship with a child; you are removing a barrier that should never have been there.

  • A full year of tuition, books and materials for one child.
  • Named scholarships can carry your family or company name.
  • You may pause or end a sponsorship at any time.
