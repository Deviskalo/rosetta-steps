Isaac’s story: the only one in his family in school

When the Rosetta Steps team first met Isaac, he was struggling. His parents could not afford to send him to school. Thanks to a scholarship, he started with us in Grade 1.

He is now in Grade 4 — with the second-best marks in the school. Isaac is the only one in his family in a classroom, and he hopes that one day his two brothers will also receive scholarships so they can join him.
