Who we are

A school built around every child's possibility

Rosetta Steps Education Center began in 2014, when Ama Harris turned her mother's childhood home into a reading room for children in the Duala community. The school opened two years later and is named for Ama's mother, Rosetta Benson Harris — a much-loved Liberian educator.

We teach the Revised Liberian National Curriculum combined with the Common Core State Standards, all in English and adapted to the lives of our students. Classes are interactive and warm: every child is known by name, and the doors stay open long after the last bell.

Our MissionTo empower and educate as many Liberian youth and families in need as we can — because education is a human right and the best means of combatting inequality, poverty and discrimination.
Our VisionA Liberia where the potential for greatness in every child is recognised, nurtured and given room to grow.
“We believe that the children of Liberia all have the potential for greatness.” Rosetta Steps Education Center
