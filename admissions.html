Admissions

Admissions open for the new school year

Places are available from Preschool (ABC1 and ABC2) through Kindergarten and Grades 1 to 6. We admit on a rolling basis where space allows, and we work hard to make sure a family's income never decides whether a child can attend.

The enrolment process

Five clear steps from first question to first day

No queues, no confusion. Here is exactly what happens, and roughly how long each step takes.

1

Submit Inquiry

Send the inquiry form below, call the office or message us on WhatsApp. We reply within two school days.

2

Visit School

Tour the campus, meet a class teacher and watch a reading lesson. Bring your child if you can.

3

Complete Application

Return the application form with your child's birth certificate, two passport photos and any previous school report.

4

Assessment

A friendly readiness conversation for little ones, or a short reading and numeracy check for older children. It places them well; it is not a test to pass.

5

Admission Decision

We confirm the place, any fee support and the start date in writing. You accept by returning the signed offer.

Most families complete the process in two to three weeks during term time.

What to bring

Everything you need for the application

  • Completed application form — available at the office or by request.
  • Child's birth certificate or age declaration.
  • Two recent passport-size photographs.
  • Previous school report or transfer certificate, if applicable.
  • Vaccination record, if you have one.
  • Parent or guardian phone number and an emergency contact.

Missing a document? Talk to us. We would rather start a conversation than have a family wait.

Fees & support

USD $280 for a full school year

The average cost of educating one child for a school year is USD $280 — less than a dollar a day. Some families can pay it; most cannot, so scholarships and fee support are assessed confidentially with each family.
