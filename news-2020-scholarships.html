Meet our 2020 scholarship recipients

Announcements2020

Eight children began the 2020 school year on full scholarship, from Grade 1 through Grade 5.

Each of them had been identified by our teachers and the wider Caldwell community as a child who would otherwise not be in a classroom. Their places were covered by donors who chose a specific child and followed them through the year.

Who the scholarships reached

The group spanned the school, from a brand-new Grade 1 pupil learning letter sounds to a Grade 5 student preparing for the transition to secondary school. The spread was deliberate. Support in the earliest grades prevents a child from falling behind at all; support in the last primary year is what keeps a child from stopping at Grade 5.

  • Eight full scholarships, Grade 1 to Grade 5
  • Tuition, uniform, materials and a daily meal for each child
  • After-school reading support in the reading room
  • Termly reports shared with each sponsor

What a full scholarship means

Our standard annual cost per child is $280. It covers tuition, a uniform, exercise books and pencils, and a meal each school day. Sponsors who prefer to give monthly can cover a place at $40 a month; those who want to support a classroom can give $125 or $200 toward a group.

Because the school opened its own campus in 2016, every scholarship has a visible home: a classroom, a reading room and a kitchen, rather than a promise.
