Dr. Mark Sinke trains our teachers

Events20 July 2023

A visit from Empowerment Squared Canada left our staff with new techniques and the school with a working printer.

Dr. Mark Sinke spent time with our teaching team on classroom practice — how to check that every child is following, how to group students so the strongest readers pull the others along, and how to plan a lesson that survives a morning without electricity.

Training that fits the room

Advice from a well-resourced school does not always survive contact with a class of forty children and one chalkboard. The sessions were built around what our teachers actually have: a single room, shared books, and children at several reading levels in the same grade.

  • Checking for understanding across a large class
  • Pairing stronger and weaker readers in every lesson
  • Low-cost materials made from what is already in the room
  • Planning for interruptions, from weather to power cuts

The printer

The visit also left a working printer behind. It sounds like a small thing until you count the cost of photocopies in Caldwell: worksheets, exam papers, report cards and letters home are all copied by hand or paid for by the page. A printer in the office means a teacher can prepare a lesson on Sunday night instead of queueing at a shop on Monday morning.

Our thanks to Dr. Sinke and to Empowerment Squared for treating teacher development as part of the donation, not an extra.
